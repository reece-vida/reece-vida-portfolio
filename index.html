My mother has taught elementary school at Lackawanna Trail for over 25 years and is about to retire; my father founded Vidaworks, a Clarks Summit digital advertising and marketing agency, in 2000 and has spent more than two decades building it into a full-service shop covering branding, web design, and digital marketing. Outside of family and work, I'm an unapologetic Roman history buff, and my music taste tends to park itself somewhere in the yacht rock era.

Goals & Plans

My path into finance started at Scranton Prep, where I was paired with a mentor who worked as a wealth advisor. That relationship is where I found what I actually love about this industry: not the headlines, but the technical, transactional work underneath it, pulling apart charts and data, building the numbers out, and then sitting across from a client and explaining exactly what it means for them. Going forward, I'm looking for that same kind of environment, small enough that I actually know and trust the people I work with, but with the real responsibilities of a middle-market shop. I want to keep taking on more and keep learning for as long as I'm doing this.

I'm excited to keep learning, keep earning real responsibility, and earn my respect in the financial world one step at a time.
